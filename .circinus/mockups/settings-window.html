Spending points
Each level makes a psycast 5% stronger
Raises its damage, the size of its area and how long its effect lasts.
Highest level one psycast can reach: 10
The Convergence specialization adds five more on top of this.
Psycaster levels needed for each psycast level: 3
Stops you pouring every point into one psycast early on. At 3, a level 30 psycaster can take a single psycast to level 10.
Psycaster stats go up on their own✓
Neural heat, heat recovery and psychic sensitivity rise with every psycaster level, so your points stay free for psycasts. Turn this off to get the "Upgrade Psycaster Stats" button back.
Links between psycasts
Psycasts feed each other✓
Psycasts in the same tree are linked. Levelling one adds a little to the ones it feeds, and the skill card shows you which.
Each linked level adds 2%
How much one level of a linked psycast gives to the psycast it feeds.
What levels change
Damage and power✓
Melee hits, explosions and anything else measured as raw power.
Size of the area✓
How far an area effect reaches.
How long effects last✓
Buffs, debuffs and anything else with a timer on it.

The psycast tree
Hide psycasts you have not learned✓
An unlearned psycast shows a question mark instead of its icon, so you find out what each one is when you take it. The shape of the tree still shows.
Still show the ones you can pick next✓
Psycasts you already meet the requirements for stay visible, so your next choice is always in front of you. Only what sits deeper up the tree is hidden.
Hide trees this pawn has not unlocked✓
The tree list shows only what the pawn owns instead of a wall of dark tiles. Works while new trees come from awakening cards, and dev mode still shows everything.
Level up effects✓
Sparks and a soft sound when you spend a point, and a gold flare when a psycast reaches its top level.
Meditation bars in the psycast tab✓
Small bars for coma risk, progress to the next psycaster level, and the climb to the next tier.
No taking it back
No refunding psycast levels 
Removes the "Reset skills" button from the psycast tab. Points you spend are spent for good.
No refunding specializations 
Removes the refund button from the constellation window.
Speed
Remember recent calculations✓
Keeps the mod cheap to run, and numbers still update the instant anything changes. Leave this on. Turn it off only if a number on screen looks wrong and you want to rule this out.
